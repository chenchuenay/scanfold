Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the ScanFold application ("ScanFold", "we", "us") handles information. Our core principle is simple: your files remain on your device by default.

Information We Do Not Collect

ScanFold is designed to process files locally on your device. We do not operate an account system, and we do not provide cloud storage for your content. Specifically, we do not collect or transmit:

  • Your documents, photos, PDFs, or ZIP files. These are processed on your device and are not uploaded by ScanFold.
  • An account or profile. ScanFold does not require registration or login for its core tools.
  • A username, email, or password associated with a ScanFold account.

If you choose to share a file (for example, through your device's share sheet, WhatsApp, or email), that action is performed by you and by your device — not by our servers.

Permissions We Request

Camera

ScanFold uses the camera only when you choose to scan a QR code, barcode, or document. The camera feed is used on-device to complete the scan and is not recorded, transmitted, or stored by us beyond the image you choose to keep.

Photos / Media

ScanFold requests access to photos only when you choose a photo from your gallery to compress, resize, convert, or include in a PDF. We access only the files you select.

Storage

Files you create in ScanFold are saved locally on your device so you can access them later from the History section.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect your personal content or account data, there is no personal information to use for advertising, analytics, or profiling. All processing occurs locally on your device to provide the features you select.

Advertising and SDKs

ScanFold does not currently integrate advertising software development kits (SDKs) that collect personal data. If we introduce advertising in the future, we will update this policy and comply with applicable platform and advertising network policies, such as Google Play's policies and recognized advertising standards.

Security

Optional password protection you enable on a PDF or ZIP is applied using device-side encryption and stored only within the file you create. Keep your password safe; if it is lost, the file cannot be unlocked.

Children's Privacy

ScanFold is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will note the updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at admin@qagenies.com.
