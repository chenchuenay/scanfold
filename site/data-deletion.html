Summary

ScanFold does not create an account and does not store your files on our servers. Your files are stored only on your device. Because of this design, deleting your data is simple and fully under your control.

How to Delete Your Files

All files you create in ScanFold are saved locally. To remove them:

1. In the app: Open History, and either delete an individual file or use the "Clear history" option to remove all files at once.

2. Off the device: Because files are stored on your device, you can also uninstall ScanFold or manually delete the files from your device's storage using its built-in files app.

No Server-Side Data

Since ScanFold has no account system and no cloud storage, there is no server-side user data to delete on our end. Nothing is transmitted to us when you use the core tools.
